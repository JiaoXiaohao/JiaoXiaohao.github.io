Research Scientist

Operational Fire Danger Products

The Research Scientist develops and evaluates daily wildfire danger products, including Burning Index, Fire Weather Index, and related operational indicators.

Research Interests

  • Fire danger index production and validation.
  • Model evaluation across regions and seasons.
  • Processing pipelines for daily geospatial products.
  • Uncertainty documentation for operational datasets.

Selected Responsibilities

  • Maintain fire danger model workflows.
  • Review daily product metadata and quality checks.
  • Coordinate research outputs with the data engineering workflow.
