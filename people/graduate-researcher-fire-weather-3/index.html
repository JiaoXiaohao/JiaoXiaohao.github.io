Fire Weather and Remote Sensing

Research Interests

  • Vegetation stress and fuel condition indicators.
  • Remote sensing for wildfire danger monitoring.

Selected Responsibilities

  • Support validation of daily raster layers.
  • Prepare figures and summaries for research updates.
