Graduate Researcher

Shanmin Fang

Fire Weather and Remote Sensing

Shanmin studies fire-weather relationships, vegetation stress, and remotely sensed indicators that help describe changing wildfire danger. The work supports daily raster evaluation and interpretation for fire danger mapping.
