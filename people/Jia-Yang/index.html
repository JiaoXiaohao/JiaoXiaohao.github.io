Principal Investigator

Dr. Jia Yang

Assistant Professor, Natural Resource Ecology and Management, Oklahoma State University

Dr. Yang studies ecosystem patterns and dynamics in response to climate change and wildfires. His work combines process-based ecosystem modeling, satellite remote sensing, and geospatial analysis to understand wildfire impacts, ecosystem biogeochemical cycles, and water resources availability.

  • Ecosystem Modeling
  • Remote Sensing
  • Wildfire Impacts
  • Water Resources

Research Interests

  • Wildfire impacts and fire danger under changing climate.
  • Ecosystem biogeochemical cycles and carbon-water interactions.
  • Water resources availability and ecohydrology.
  • Process-based ecosystem modeling.
  • Satellite remote sensing, GIS, and geospatial data modeling.

Education and Previous Positions

  • Ph.D. in Forestry, Auburn University.
  • B.S. in Biology and Applied Meteorology, China Agricultural University.
