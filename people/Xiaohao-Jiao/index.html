Xiaohao Jiao

Graduate Research Assistant, Wildfire Modeling and Risk Assessment

Xiaohao Jiao studies wildfire modeling and risk assessment using data-driven approaches, including machine learning, remote sensing, and field observations. His work focuses on understanding wildfire behavior, evaluating fire risk, and examining how fires interact with environmental conditions across changing landscapes.
