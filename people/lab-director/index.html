Dr. Lab Director

Professor of Fire Science and Earth System Modeling

Dr. Lab Director leads the laboratory’s research on wildfire danger forecasting, climate extremes, and geospatial decision support. Their work connects operational fire danger products with scientific questions about climate, fuels, weather, and landscape risk.

Research Interests

  • Wildfire danger forecasting and early warning.
  • Climate extremes and fire-weather interactions.
  • Geospatial decision support for partners and agencies.
  • Reproducible research workflows for operational raster products.

Selected Responsibilities

  • Laboratory strategy and research supervision.
  • External collaborations and partner engagement.
  • Scientific oversight for daily fire danger products.
