Research Linking Ecosystem Processes, Fire, Climate, and Water

The laboratory combines process-based ecosystem modeling, satellite remote sensing, and geospatial data analysis to study wildfire impacts, ecosystem biogeochemical cycles, water resources availability, and climate-change adaptation.

Current Focus

  • Ecosystem patterns and dynamics under climate change and wildfire.
  • Process-based ecosystem modeling for carbon, water, and vegetation processes.
  • Satellite remote sensing and geospatial data modeling.
  • Wildfire impacts, fire danger, and Southern Great Plains landscape change.

Featured Tool

The fire danger index portal provides a public map interface for daily GeoTIFF layers such as Burning Index, Fire Weather Index, and Wildland Fire Likelihood.

Research Workflow

From Environmental Observations to Ecosystem Insight

The lab integrates satellite observations, climate and weather data, process-based models, and reproducible geospatial workflows to study ecosystems under climate and wildfire disturbance.
