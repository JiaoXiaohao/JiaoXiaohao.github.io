About

The Wildfire Risk and Earth System Laboratory is led by Dr. Jia Yang in the Department of Natural Resource Ecology and Management at Oklahoma State University. The lab studies ecosystem patterns and dynamics in response to climate change and wildfires, using process-based ecosystem modeling, satellite remote sensing, and geospatial data analysis.

Mission

Our mission is to improve understanding of how climate, fire, vegetation, water, and biogeochemical cycles interact across landscapes. The lab connects field-relevant natural resource questions with reproducible modeling and remote-sensing workflows.

Research Areas

  • Wildfire impacts and fire danger under changing climate.
  • Ecosystem biogeochemical cycles, including carbon and water exchanges.
  • Water resources availability and ecohydrology.
  • Satellite remote sensing, GIS, modeling, and simulation.
