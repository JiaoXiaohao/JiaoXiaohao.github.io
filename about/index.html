About

The Wildfire Risk and Earth System Laboratory develops science, data products, and open workflows for understanding and communicating wildfire danger. We work across fire weather, climate extremes, remote sensing, and geospatial information systems.

Mission

Our mission is to connect daily wildfire danger monitoring with rigorous research. We aim to make scientific products easier to inspect, reproduce, cite, and share with partners.

Website Maintenance

The website is built with Hexo and a small custom theme. Most content is stored as Markdown under source/. Fire danger products are stored under source/fire-danger-data/, and the map reads manifest.json generated by the update script.

Citation

When citing daily data products, include the product name, date, processing version, laboratory name, and access date. If a DOI is later assigned to archived products, use the DOI in addition to the web page URL.
