Wildfire Danger Index

The portal displays daily laboratory-produced GeoTIFF layers for wildfire danger monitoring. Supported products include Burning Index, Fire Weather Index, Wildland Fire Likelihood, and related operational fire danger metrics. Use the dashboard to inspect layer metadata, compare product availability, and download one or more GeoTIFF files.

Data Policy

Each raster layer should be accompanied by clear metadata: index name, production date, units, spatial extent, processing version, and contact point. For operational use, always check the timestamp and notes before making decisions.

Supported Products

  • BI: Burning Index.
  • FWI: Fire Weather Index.
  • WFL: Wildland Fire Likelihood.
  • ERC: Energy Release Component.
  • SC: Spread Component.
  • IC: Ignition Component.
