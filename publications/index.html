Publications

Selected publications below are drawn from Dr. Jia Yang’s Oklahoma State University Experts profile. Lab members are shown in bold where author information is visible in the public listing.

2026

Satellite-based analysis of hourly progression and driving factors of large U.S. wildfires

Fang S, Yang J, Jiao X, et al. 2026. International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation 149.

This study develops an hourly wildfire progression dataset for large U.S. wildfires and analyzes environmental drivers of rapid spread.

DOI Related Theme

Maturation of encroaching juniper woodland elevates gross primary productivity and water use but reduces net ecosystem exchange relative to native tallgrass prairie

Zhang T, Zou CB, Will RE, et al. 2026. Agricultural and Forest Meteorology 378.

The paper compares carbon and water fluxes between mature juniper woodland and adjacent tallgrass prairie in the Southern Great Plains.

DOI Related Theme

Increasing irrigation water requirements across croplands in the Contiguous United States throughout the 21st century

Yang H, Yang J, Ochsner TE, et al. 2026. Agricultural Water Management 323.

The study estimates future crop-specific irrigation water requirements across the contiguous United States under climate-change scenarios.

DOI Related Theme

2025

Do forests store more water? A comparative analysis of water storage between multiple land cover types

Wu Q, Yang J, Song J, et al. 2025. Journal of Hydrology 661.

This article compares water storage capacity across land cover types using surface water and groundwater budget analysis.

DOI Related Theme

Generating a downscaled hourly surface soil moisture dataset for wildfire prediction

Yang H, Wang Q, Yang J, et al. 2025. Journal of Hydrology 661.

The study downscales hourly surface soil moisture data and evaluates its value for wildfire prediction across the continental United States.

DOI Related Theme

A 3 h, 1 km surface soil moisture dataset for the contiguous United States from 2015 to 2023

Yang H, Yang J, Ochsner TE, et al. 2025. Earth System Science Data 17(7):3391-3409.

This data paper presents a long time-series, spatially seamless surface soil moisture dataset at 3-hour and 1-km resolution.

DOI Related Theme

Wildfire danger under changing climates in the southern Great Plains throughout the 21st century

Fang S, Yang J, Zou CB, et al. 2025. Ecological Indicators 170.

The paper projects wildfire danger changes in the Southern Great Plains and identifies key weather drivers of future risk.

Yang J, Will R, Zhai L, et al. 2024. Earth's Future 12(7).

High-resolution Annual Dynamic dataset of Curve Number from 2008 to 2021 over Conterminous United States

DOI Related Theme

Ouyang Y, Yang J, Huang Y, et al. 2024. Agricultural Water Management 302.
