Decision Support and Open Data

Metadata, web maps, reproducibility, and partner tools

We build transparent tools for sharing fire risk information with researchers, land managers, and partner agencies. Our products emphasize metadata, versioned processing, and clear uncertainty communication.

Core Questions

  • What metadata is needed for daily fire danger products to be trusted and reused?
  • How can static websites publish operational geospatial products without complex infrastructure?
  • Which visual and textual summaries help users interpret wildfire danger responsibly?

Methods

  • Metadata-driven raster publishing.
  • Static web maps for daily GeoTIFF products.
  • Documentation and deployment automation.
  • Partner feedback and usability review.

Outputs

  • Interactive fire danger map pages.
  • Deployment and data update documentation.
  • Open workflows for reproducible product publication.
