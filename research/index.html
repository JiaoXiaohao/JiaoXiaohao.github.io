Research

Our group studies ecosystem patterns and dynamics in response to climate change and wildfires. We combine process-based ecosystem modeling, satellite remote sensing, GIS, and geospatial data modeling to understand carbon, water, vegetation, and fire processes across landscapes.

01 Wildfire Impacts and Fire Danger Fire progression, fire danger, climate drivers, and ecosystem response.

Modeling and satellite-based analysis of wildfire dynamics, fire danger, and ecological impacts under changing climate conditions.

 02 Climate Change and Ecosystem Dynamics Ecosystem patterns, adaptation, vegetation shifts, and climate extremes.

Studying how climate change alters ecosystem structure, productivity, fire regimes, and natural resource resilience.

 03 Satellite Remote Sensing and Geospatial Modeling Soil moisture, vegetation, fuels, burned areas, and spatial data products.

Using satellite observations and GIS to map environmental drivers, ecosystem change, and wildfire-relevant landscape conditions.

 04 Water Resources and Biogeochemical Cycles Carbon-water exchanges, evapotranspiration, ecohydrology, and resource availability.

Quantifying how land cover, vegetation change, and climate influence water availability and ecosystem biogeochemical cycling.
