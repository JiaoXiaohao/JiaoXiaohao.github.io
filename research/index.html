Research

Our group studies wildfire as a coupled climate, vegetation, weather, and human-system problem. We combine physical modeling, remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and operational data workflows to support scientific understanding and practical risk management.

Research Themes

01 Fire Danger and Operational Indices Daily products, model evaluation, and operational workflows.

Burning Index, Fire Weather Index, Wildland Fire Likelihood, and related fire-weather metrics for preparedness and research.

 02 Fire Weather and Climate Extremes Heat, drought, wind, humidity, and climate variability.

Understanding how short-term weather and long-term climate shape wildfire danger, timing, and severity.

 03 Remote Sensing of Fuels and Burned Areas Vegetation condition, fuel proxies, and post-fire recovery.

Satellite-driven workflows for monitoring landscape conditions and evaluating fire impacts across regions.

 04 Decision Support and Open Data Metadata, web maps, reproducibility, and partner tools.

Transparent systems for publishing fire danger products, documenting uncertainty, and supporting collaboration.
