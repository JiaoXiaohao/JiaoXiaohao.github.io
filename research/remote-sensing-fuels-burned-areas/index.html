Satellite Remote Sensing and Geospatial Modeling

Soil moisture, vegetation, fuels, burned areas, and spatial data products

Satellite observations help us characterize environmental conditions that affect ecosystem processes and wildfire behavior. The lab uses remote sensing and geospatial modeling to build spatial datasets, analyze fire progression, and evaluate land surface conditions at fine spatial and temporal scales.

Core Questions

  • How can satellite observations improve wildfire prediction and ecosystem monitoring?
  • How do soil moisture, vegetation condition, and land surface temperature vary across space and time?
  • How can remote sensing products be fused or downscaled for regional and continental studies?

Methods

  • Satellite data fusion and downscaling.
  • GIS and geospatial data modeling.
  • Fire progression analysis from geostationary satellite products.
  • Validation against in situ and independent observation networks.

Outputs

  • High-resolution soil moisture and hydrologic datasets.
  • Satellite-based wildfire progression analyses.
  • Geospatial products for ecosystem and fire-risk modeling.
