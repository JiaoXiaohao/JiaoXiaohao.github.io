Remote Sensing of Fuels and Burned Areas

Vegetation condition, fuel proxies, and post-fire recovery

Satellite observations help us characterize vegetation condition, fuel moisture proxies, burned area patterns, and post-fire recovery. We focus on workflows that can be reproduced across regions and updated as new imagery becomes available.

Core Questions

  • How do vegetation condition and fuel proxies relate to daily fire danger?
  • Which remote-sensing products are most useful for operational monitoring?
  • How can burned area and recovery patterns inform future risk assessment?

Methods

  • Satellite data preprocessing and quality screening.
  • Fuel and vegetation stress proxy development.
  • Burned area mapping and post-fire recovery summaries.
  • Raster integration with daily fire danger products.

Outputs

  • Vegetation and fuel condition layers.
  • Burned area and recovery summaries.
  • Remote-sensing inputs for model evaluation.
