Climate Change and Ecosystem Dynamics

Ecosystem patterns, adaptation, vegetation shifts, and climate extremes

We investigate how climate change affects ecosystem patterns and dynamics, including productivity, vegetation distribution, wildfire risk, and resource availability. This work connects climate projections with ecosystem models and spatial observations.

Core Questions

  • How do ecosystems respond to warming, drought, and changing fire regimes?
  • How will woody plant encroachment and vegetation ranges shift under future climate?
  • Which climate drivers most strongly affect ecosystem water and carbon exchange?

Methods

  • Climate projection analysis.
  • Species distribution and ecosystem modeling.
  • Time-series analysis of temperature, water, vegetation, and carbon dynamics.
  • Spatial assessment of climate impacts and adaptation questions.

Outputs

  • Studies of climate-change impacts and adaptation.
  • Projections of vegetation and woody plant range shifts.
  • Analyses of ecosystem carbon-water responses under climate stress.
