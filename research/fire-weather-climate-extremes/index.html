Fire Weather and Climate Extremes

Heat, drought, wind, humidity, and climate variability

We investigate how heat waves, drought, wind, humidity, and atmospheric circulation influence the timing and severity of wildfire danger. This work links daily meteorology with longer-term climate variability and change.

Core Questions

  • Which combinations of weather variables drive high-danger days?
  • How do climate extremes alter the seasonal window of fire danger?
  • How should operational products communicate changing baseline risk?

Methods

  • Fire-weather diagnostics using daily meteorological fields.
  • Extreme-event analysis for heat, drought, wind, and humidity.
  • Seasonal and interannual comparison of danger indices.
  • Statistical summaries for partner-facing interpretation.

Outputs

  • Fire-weather driver summaries.
  • Climate-extreme case studies.
  • Seasonal fire danger interpretation notes.
